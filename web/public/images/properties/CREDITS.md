# Property photos

Demo photos for the seed data, from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use, no attribution required; credited anyway).
Cropped to 960×640 and saved as WebP, plus a 480×320 copy for cards and thumbnails (`*-480.webp`).
The seed picks a main photo per listing by property type (`api/db/seeds/property_generator.rb`)
and a gallery of rooms it has (`kitchen-*`, `living-*`, `bedroom-*`, `bathroom-*`, `yard-*`, in
`api/db/seeds/listing_extras.rb`).
